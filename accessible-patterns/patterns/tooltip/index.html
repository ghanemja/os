Tooltip

A short description that appears when a control gets keyboard focus or the pointer hovers over it. The tooltip is linked with aria-describedby, so screen readers read it after the control's name.

Copies the page URL to your clipboard Drafts are kept for 30 days

Try it

  • Tab to a button. The tooltip appears right away.
  • Hover with the mouse, then move onto the tooltip. It stays open (WCAG 1.4.13, hoverable).
  • Press Escape to hide it without moving focus (dismissible).

Tooltips are for supplementary information only. Never put essential content or interactive elements in them, since touch users may never see them.

Follows the WAI-ARIA APG tooltip pattern.
